# Geophysics references

Written by `tools/geophysics_reference/generate.py` under `.venv-pet` (Python 3.12, NumPy 2.5.3). That environment has SimulatorWrap (`subsurface`) at `d29dc57`, checked byte for byte by sha256, PyLops 2.8.0 and bruges 0.5.4. APEX imports none of them. Each case's generator is seeded, and its seed is stored in `meta_seed`.

- `reflectivity.npz` (seed 20261013): 240 random sedimentary interfaces (Vp 1500–4500 m/s, Vs/Vp 0.3–0.65, density 1800–2700 kg/m³, a third with contrasts within 3 %), none evanescent, at 0, 5, 10, 20, 30 and 40°. It holds PyLops' `approx_zoeppritz_pp` (as SimulatorWrap calls it), `zoeppritz_pp`, and the `akirichards` and `fatti` coefficients; and bruges' `zoeppritz_rpp`, `zoeppritz`, `akirichards`, `fatti` and `shuey`. Outputs are `(n_angle, n_interface)`. Used by `tests/unit/algorithms/test_geophysics_reflectivity.py`.
- `pem_standard.npz` (seed 20261014): SimulatorWrap `standardrp.elasticproperties.calc_props` on 200 cells with oil, water and gas. Porosity is 0.05–0.35, with four cells at 0.40–0.44 above φc where the reference extrapolates; pressure is 10–40 MPa and overburden 40–60 MPa, with four cells below their pressure. Inputs are in MPa, as the reference takes them. Outputs are Vp, Vs, density and the saturated moduli (MPa). Used by `tests/parity/test_geophysics_pem.py`.
- `pem_soft_sand.npz` (seed 20261015): `softsandrp.elasticproperties.calc_props` on 200 brine–CO₂ cells, with densities given, and porosity up to 0.355 plus six cells at 0.36–0.6, at and above φc, where the reference's cap holds the rock. It also holds, per cell, the reference's pore fluid (`_fluid_dens`, `_fluidprops_Brie`), Hertz-Mindlin and dry-rock moduli, its clamped effective pressure, and its gas (CO₂, methane), water and brine moduli. Same test file.
- `rock_physics_bruges.npz` (seed 20261016): bruges' `hashin_shtrikman`, `hertz_mindlin`, `soft_sand`, `smith_gassmann`, `wood`, `v_water` and `v_brine` on 100 random inputs. Used by `tests/unit/algorithms/test_geophysics_rock_physics.py`. bruges' shear bounds have a defect (pet_parity.md, B1).
- `avo_traces.npz` (seed 20261017): SimulatorWrap `flow_avo._calc_avo_props_active_cells` on a 7×6×5 all-active grid with the 3dBox AVO case's PET settings. It holds one call per angle (10° and 20°) and one call with both, which returns the 20° traces twice (pet_parity.md G7). Inputs are `(nz, ny, nx)` with the top depth `(ny, nx)`; outputs are `(ny, nx, n_time)`. Used by `tests/parity/test_geophysics_avo.py`.
- `flow_avo_3dbox.npz` (no seed: read from a run): SimulatorWrap's own reading of an OPM Flow run of `examples/3dbox_production_avo`'s truth with the PET deck's ln PERMX cap of 6 (`permx_cap = 6.0`; OPM Flow 2026.04, in `meta_flow_version`), as `flow_avo.get_avo_result` does at each vintage: `calc_pem` (restart arrays through PET's `misc.ecl`, `standardrp`), `calc_velocities` and `_calc_avo_props_active_cells`, one angle per call. The pressures go to `standardrp` in MPa (`press_conv = 0.1`) and the overburden is the case's PET set-up's 320 bar, so that the comparison is of reading and wiring. It holds the traces `(81, 293)` of every 7th column along I and J (`out_columns`, 0-based `(I, J)`) for 10° and 20° at days 2000 and 4000. Written by `tools/geophysics_reference/flow_avo_3dbox.py` from a run folder (its docstring gives the two commands). Used by `tests/integration/test_geophysics_3dbox.py` (measured: within 3.3e-15 of the peak).
- `flow_avo_import_3dbox.npz` (no seed: read from the same kind of run): the AVO observations SimulatorWrap's `flow_avo.get_avo_result` writes for that truth, run whole as PET runs it: a fixed baseline at the start (without one it fails, G10), vintages at days 2000 and 4000, 20° only (G7), the masked differences flattened (`out_<variant>_<days>`), for three variants of its mask (`in_<variant>_avo`, the `avo` keys): `fallback` (its default ellipse, whose completions are in an include it does not read, so it surrounds every active column), `ellipse` (300 m around the completions written inline into the deck), `rectangle` (`x_range`/`y_range`), each thinned by `step_x`/`step_y`. The time axis is the reservoir's first 0.1 s, to keep the file small. Written by `tools/geophysics_reference/flow_avo_import_3dbox.py`; used by `tests/integration/test_geophysics_3dbox.py`, which imports each with `apex pet-import` and forecasts the same run.
- `potential_3dbox.npz` (no seed: read from a run): SimulatorWrap's own gravity and seafloor displacement of an OPM Flow run of `examples/3dbox_production_avo`'s truth with the PET deck's ln PERMX cap of 6 (`permx_cap = 6.0`; OPM Flow 2026.04, in `meta_flow_version`; the deck writes DEN and RPORV): `flow_grav.get_grav_result` and `flow_seafloor_disp.get_displacement_result` (Geertsma, `integral` mode, ν = 0.25, compressibility 1e-3 per MPa, methods `pressure` and `porv`, pressures in MPa by `press_conv = 0.1`) with `meas_spacing` 600 m (25 stations, 742.5 m apart), 600 m of padding, 300 m deep, a baseline at the start and vintages at days 2000 and 4000. It holds the stations (`out_stations`), Δg (`out_grav_<days>`, µGal), u_z (`out_disp_<method>_<days>`, cm), and the per-cell mass changes (`out_grav_dm_<days>`, the sum of its saved per-phase changes) and volume decreases (`out_disp_dv_<method>_<days>`, computed by the driver with `map_z_response`'s formula from the pore volumes and MPa pressures the reference keeps in `dyn_var`), with `meta_aquifer_filtered` (False: its aquifer filter removed no cell). Written by `tools/geophysics_reference/flow_potential_3dbox.py`; used by `tests/integration/test_geophysics_3dbox.py`.
- `nist_co2.csv` (no seed: published values): CO₂'s density and speed of sound at 13 states (20–100 °C, 5–40 MPa: the near-critical region, both branches below the critical temperature, and the storage range) and on the saturation curve (liquid and vapour at 25.0 and 29.95 °C), from the NIST Chemistry WebBook's fluid tables (Span & Wagner 1996), retrieved 2026-10-14; the file's header gives the source. Used by `tests/unit/algorithms/test_geophysics_co2.py` against CoolProp 8.0.0 and APEX's table.
- `stations.npz` (seed 20261020): SimulatorWrap `measurement_locations` on a slanted 6 × 5 × 3 grid (`in_coord`, `in_zcorn`, `in_actnum`, its cell centres `in_centres`): `out_plain`, `out_refined` (finer within 400 m of a well) and `out_seabed` (a seabed table, `in_seabed`), each `(n_station, 3)`.
- `gravity.npz` (seed 20261021): SimulatorWrap `flow_grav.calc_grav` (µGal, G = 6.67e-11) of three phases' mass changes on that kind of grid: `in_dm` per active cell, `in_stations`, `out_dg`, and the reference's cell centres `out_centres`.
- `seafloor_geertsma.npz` (seed 20261022): SimulatorWrap `compute_deformation_transfer` (Geertsma, `integral` mode, ν = 0.25, ΔV = 1) for 6 cells × 12 stations in three sets: `close` (its cut-off negligible), `truncated` (stations to 6 depths, zero beyond 3) and `wide` (to 3 depths), which measure G12.
